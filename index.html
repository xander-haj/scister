Information

  • Upload an image and perform OCR.
  • Use the drawing or cropping tools to select areas for OCR or to crop the image.
  • After drawing a crop rectangle, click "Confirm Crop" to apply the crop.
  • Recognized words within drawn rectangles will be highlighted in light blue on the image.
  • Click on a highlighted block to assign the recognized text to any input field.
  • Press "Update Table" to add a row with the current field values.
  • Process multiple images by clearing the image and fields.
  • Finally, press "Save CSV" to download the collected data.
