Information

  • Upload an image by dragging and dropping it into the designated area or by clicking to select a file.
  • Once uploaded, click "Open Editor" to start editing the image.
  • Use the toolbar to select specific areas for OCR or to crop the image.
  • After drawing a crop rectangle, click "Confirm Crop" to apply the crop.
  • Recognized text within drawn rectangles will be highlighted in blue on the image.
  • Click on a highlighted rectangle to assign the extracted text to one of the input fields.
  • Once done, press "Update Table" to add a row to the table.
  • Press "Save CSV" to download all collected data in CSV format.
